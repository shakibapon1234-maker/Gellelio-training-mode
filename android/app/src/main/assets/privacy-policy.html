Gellelio Galileo Training Simulator — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 September 2026

Gellelio is an offline educational practice simulator. It does not connect to a live GDS and is not affiliated with Travelport or Galileo.

Information we collect

Gellelio does not collect, sell, or transmit personal information. Practice commands and simulated PNR data are processed locally on the device.

Network access

The simulator does not require an internet connection for training. Desktop and Android builds do not send command history, passenger data, or license information to a remote server.

Local storage

Any future license activation data will be stored locally on the licensed device. Students should use fictional training data and must not enter real passenger or payment information.

Children and students

This software is intended for academy training. Instructors are responsible for providing appropriate guidance and fictional practice data.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Wings Fly Aviation Academy through the academy's official support channel.
